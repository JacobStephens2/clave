# Concept Brief: Clave – A Partner Dance Game (Salsa)

**One-line pitch:** Dance salsa with a partner in an Android digital card game where you telegraph, position, move, flourish, and shine to share the music in a way that feels connected, rhythmic, and joyful.

**Elevator pitch (2–3 sentences):**
<What is it, who is it for, why is it fun?>
This is a digital card game especially for beginner to intermediate salsa dancers, who will enjoy it because it will let them see a wider variety of dance dynamics and moves work together in such a way that will show new moves and possibilities in salsa dance that may pique their curiosity and develop their understanding of real life salsa. There's an element of discovery in the kind of learning in this system. Secondarily, they will enjoy it because they will get to have the fantasy of having great salsa dances, even when they don't fully have the technical skill to do in person everything they can do in the game.

### 1. Dance as the subject matter
- Every card is a real move. The vocabulary is authentic social dance, consulted with real dancers, usable as a teaching tool for people who've never danced.
### 2. Play experience
- Tabletop, not animation. Clave plays like a digital board game — the pleasure is in state and strategy, not in watching sprites. This keeps scope focused and the art direction warm and stylized rather than visually ambitious.
### 3. Systems and mechanics
- Mistakes are part of the dance. Breaks, recoveries, and awkward moments aren't failures — they're gameplay. The resolution ladder (Perfect -> Clean -> Awkward -> Recovered -> Break) means "imperfect" outcomes have their own texture and scoring, not just "you lose points." This mirrors real social dancing, where recovery is a skill.
### 4. Expansion and Longevity
- One vocabulary, many dialects. Clave is the first in the Partner Dance Game series, so its design choices should have potential to work with other dance styles later, such as Lindy Hop, Foxtrot, and Fusion.
- The song is the match. Each piece of music is its own game unit — with its own tempo, structure, and mood. The game's longevity comes from the infinite variety of music, not from procedural content or grind loops.

## Non-Goals
- <What this game will NOT be>
- <Scope boundaries>
- This game will not be a CCG fighter with a dance skin.
- This game's scope will not expand to include dancer position on a dance floor map

## Target Player
- Platform: Android phones primarily and iOS phones secondarily
- Audience: Gamey salsa dancers, such as my wife and I
- Comparable titles: Lindyhop: The Card Game, Fox in the Forest Duet, Hanabi, Sky Team, Regicide

## Core Fantasy
<What does the player feel they are doing?>
Dancing a social salsa dance where they can hit great connection to their partner and the music, and do moves and patterns they don't normally do or haven't learned in body yet.

## Success Criteria
- <Prototype goal> One playable song section
- <Vertical slice goal> One playable song
- <Launch goal> An album's worth of playable songs and enough content to introduce new cards in each song.
