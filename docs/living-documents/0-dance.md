### The Five Canonical Sections

"Llorarás" divides cleanly into **5 structural parts** — intro, three cuerpo verses with returning chorus hook, and a closing pregón/mambo. Below is the full bar-by-bar map. Each bar = one 4-beat measure (equivalent to one full basic step for a dancer stepping on 1 or on 2). [spanishconsalsa](https://www.spanishconsalsa.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/09/Lloraras.pdf)

## Song Section description

### Introducion

| Bars | Element | What Happens |
|------|---------|--------------|
| 1–9 | **Bass tumbao, piano montuno + congas enter entry** | Oscar D'León's iconic walking bassline establishes the groove alone — Am → Dm → E7 cycle begins, piano guajeo locks to the 2-3 son clave; tumbao firmly established |
| 10–24 | **Trombones enter with signature riff, Full band groove / pickup to verse** | Sharp, repetitive brass stab introduces the melodic hook that will return in every montuno  |

***

### Mambo

Instrumental climax. Trombones take over with a **syncopated, layered horn arrangement (moña)**. The sonero drops out briefly or shouts punctuations. Percussion breaks open — timbales solo fills, conga flourishes. [observationblogger](https://observationblogger.com/2026/01/03/lloraras-1974-oscar-dleon/)

| Bars | Element |
|------|---------|
| 161–168 | First moña: trombones play overlapping riff |
| 169–176 | Second moña: horns stack, key hit points on bars 4 and 8 |
| 177–184 | Percussion break / timbales feature |
| 185–192 | Band re-synchronizes, horns punch toward cierre |

### Pregón & Coro

This is the **heart of the song** and the longest continuous section. It's pure **coro-pregón call-and-response**: [spanishconsalsa](https://www.spanishconsalsa.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/09/Lloraras.pdf)

| Bars | Element |
|------|---------|
| 97–104 | **Coro locks in:** "Llorarás, llorarás, llorarás" (2-bar hook, repeating) |
| 105–112 | **Pregón 1:** "Como lo sufrí yo" → coro answers "Llorarás" |
| 113–120 | **Pregón 2:** "Oye, tú llorarás" → coro answers |
| 121–128 | **Pregón 3:** "Nadie te comprenderá" → coro answers |
| 129–136 | **Pregón 4:** "Todo lo malo que hiciste" → coro answers |
| 137–144 | **Pregón 5:** "Oye, mira, lo pagarás" → coro answers |
| 145–160 | **Sonero improv escalates** — Oscar ad-libs with growing intensity; percussion opens up |

***

### Cierre
Lyric: *"Ahora yo voy a vivir mi vida como yo quiera... Echa pa'lante, que me voy... En esta Navidad, mama, ¡llévatela!"* [spanishconsalsa](https://www.spanishconsalsa.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/09/Lloraras.pdf)

Oscar returns for a final burst of soneo over the coro, growing more triumphant. The phrase **"¡Llévatela!"** is his signature cue to the band to **end the song** (a common Venezuelan salsa convention for handing it to the musicians to close). Band hits a **crisp synchronized cierre** — no fade out.
