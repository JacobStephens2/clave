### GAME STRUCTURE

- **Cooperative** — The two players are a lead and a follow partners in a salsa dance, trying to have as much connection and style as possible in their dance. (Forbidden Island)

### TURN ORDER & STRUCTURE

- **Fixed turn order** — Initially there doesn't need to be role switching, so the start player can be the lead, & the second player the follow. (Fox)  
- **Sequential phases** — The player does everything on their turn that makes for — all phases: draw, play, discard, draw. (Fox)

### ACTIONS: Move / Dance, Learn

- **Action Selection Restrictions** — The first play of a dance must be a position card. This is a restriction on which cards may be played. (OPTIONAL)  
- **Matching** — After a position has been established, the move played must match the position parameter. The position cards could even be dropped, and perhaps the game could just have move matching, if move card ends in particular positions. If there is no card out, anything may be led. (Hearts). There's a risk of this all being too simple.  
- **Action Drafting** — The private lessons could be avail. only to one player per round

### RESOLUTION

At most basic, the game can function with no uncertain outcomes. Can be part of scoring, maybe.

### GAME END & VICTORY

- **Fixed Number of Rounds** — It will have 2 rounds — two phrases of 8 cards played, the cuerpo & montuno — per the song deck.  
- **Exhausted Resources** — You lose if your connection reaches 0. You lose connection if you can't follow / match your card somehow.  
- **Victory Points from Game State** — At the end of each phrase score is calculated — Style & connection.
