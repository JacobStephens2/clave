## Right page (51)

### Actions (Race for the Galaxy, Citadels)

Players / dancers could draft actions such as Step, Move, Flourish, Signal, Position. But that doesn't quite fit the theme as well — at least doesn't simulate it, as dancing, usually the follow doesn't decide the move, unless the players are more abstractly together creating this dance, and there could be an order of these on him… I'm now thinking a variant of role selection — you determine the position for that role plays first, then 1 play the Step, and then the move signal, then the move, then the flourish. In this way players could choose roles based on cards in their hand & based on what they know their partner tends to do / what their goal is… Be wary of it being that one player always flourishes for example. There could be style point bonuses for both partners to flourish though, encouraging a divide here.

Variable player powers (18xx, Through the Ages, Cosmic Encounter, Spirit Island) could be introduced at some point — different dancers the players are w/ special strengths or powers, but this is outside the core game loop & prototype scope. Or beginner cards in market / lessons & intermediate cards.

Gating & unlocking (18xx & Agricola) could be used as the song progresses, allowing new moves with new song sections, though not very thematic, as you can do any move @ any time, you just generally don't want to.

Events (Through the Ages, Monopoly) could be used to capture things that happen in the dance, such as a tight dance floor, or beer spills, or dance circle forms.

**Matching:** This is a key mechanism in Clave. To play a given move, the game state / a previously played and still active card needs to be present / on the board, such as an open position for a follow left turn, or a flourish of — a hand flourish needs half closed for example. Players — there could be a reset / recovery.
