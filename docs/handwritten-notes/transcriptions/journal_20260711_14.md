## Right page (95) — 6/15/26 Clave Rules

### # Setup

Each player assembles a starting deck of 8 cards — these cards represent moves learned in salsa beginner lessons.

- 4 open position cards  
- 1 follow right turn  
- 1 follow left turn  
- 1 lead right turn  
- 1 lead left turn  

Each player shuffles their deck and draws four cards as their starting hand.
